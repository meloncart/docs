# Features

Meloncart is a full-featured eCommerce toolkit built on Laravel and October CMS. Explore what's included.

- [Customization](./customization) — Full creative control over every aspect of your store
- [Product Management](./product-management) — A complete toolkit for managing your product catalog
- [Customer Management](./customer-management) — Tools for managing your customer base
- [Order Management](./order-management) — Track, manage and fulfill orders from a single screen
- [Payments](./payments) — Accept payments with Stripe, PayPal and more
- [Shipping](./shipping) — Flexible shipping rates, tracking and label printing
- [Checkout](./checkout) — A fully customizable checkout experience
- [Marketing & Promotion](./marketing) — SEO, discounts, email and tools to grow your store
- [Reporting & Analytics](./reporting) — Sales reports, product stats and data exports
- [Software Updates](./updates) — Simple updates that won't break your store
- [Security](./security) — Built-in security for your store, your team and your customers
- [Performance](./performance) — Built on a fast, modern stack that scales with your store
- [Customer Support](./support) — Get help through multiple channels
- [Multi-Store](./multi-store) — Run multiple storefronts from a single installation
- [Marketplace](./marketplace) — Extend your store with plugins and themes from the October CMS Marketplace
