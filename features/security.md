<div class="mc-detail-section">
    <div class="mc-detail-section__text">
        <h2>Payment Security</h2>
        <p>Meloncart never stores credit card numbers. Payment data is handled directly by your payment gateway (Stripe, PayPal, etc.) using their secure APIs, keeping card details off your server entirely.</p>
        <p>This minimizes your <strong>PCI compliance</strong> scope and reduces risk.</p>
