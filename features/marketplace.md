# Marketplace

The October CMS Marketplace provides a centralized place to find plugins, themes and integrations that extend your Meloncart store. Browse what's available or contribute your own.

## Plugins

Find additional shipping providers, payment gateways, analytics integrations and other functionality on the Marketplace. Plugins are built by both the core team and third-party developers, with new additions published regularly.

Install plugins directly from the admin panel or via Composer.

## Themes

Looking for a starting point rather than building from scratch? Browse themes on the Marketplace to get a ready-made storefront that you can customize to fit your needs.

## Build and Publish

The Marketplace is open to developers who want to distribute their own plugins and themes — free or paid. It handles licensing and distribution, so you can focus on building. If you've built something useful for your own store, consider sharing it with the community.
