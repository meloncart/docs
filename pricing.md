<div class="mc-pricing-faq">
    <div class="mc-pricing-faq__inner">
        <h2>Common Questions</h2>
        <div class="mc-pricing-faq-list">
            <details class="mc-pricing-faq-item">
                <summary>Is this a subscription?</summary>
                <p>No. Meloncart is a one-time purchase. Your license includes access to updates for as long as it's active. If you choose not to renew, your store continues to run on the last version you installed.</p>
            </details>
            <details class="mc-pricing-faq-item">
                <summary>What happens when my license expires?</summary>
                <p>Your store keeps running. You just stop receiving new updates and features. You can renew at any time to resume updates.</p>
            </details>
            <details class="mc-pricing-faq-item">
                <summary>Do I need a separate license for each store?</summary>
                <p>Each license covers one October CMS installation. The multi-store feature lets you run multiple storefronts from a single installation, so one license can power multiple stores.</p>
            </details>
            <details class="mc-pricing-faq-item">
                <summary>Is October CMS free?</summary>
                <p>The October CMS platform is free for the first year. After that, the annual renewal is optional and keeps you on the latest version. Your site continues to run regardless.</p>
            </details>
            <details class="mc-pricing-faq-item">
                <summary>What payment gateways are supported?</summary>
                <p>Stripe, PayPal, and RazorPay are included out of the box. Additional gateways can be added through the <a href="https://octobercms.com/plugins/e-commerce">Marketplace</a> or by building your own using the <a href="/developer/extending/payment-gateways">Payment Gateway API</a>.</p>
            </details>
            <details class="mc-pricing-faq-item">
                <summary>Can I try it before buying?</summary>
                <p>Yes. The <a href="https://octobercms.com/demo/meloncart">live demo</a> gives you full access to the admin panel and a working storefront so you can explore every feature.</p>
            </details>
        </div>
