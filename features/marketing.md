        <p>Templates are fully customizable from the admin panel, keeping your brand consistent from the storefront through to the package your customer receives.</p>

        <h2>Discounts and Coupons</h2>
        <p>Create flexible promotions with Meloncart's <strong>discount engine</strong>. Combine conditions like order total, shipping destination or customer group to target the right audience. For example, 10% off orders over $100 or free shipping for VIP customers.</p>
        <p>Discounts can apply to the whole cart or individual products, and coupon codes can be generated for use in marketing campaigns.</p>
