<div class="mc-features-hero">
    <h1>Checkout</h1>
    <p>Meloncart doesn't impose a fixed checkout flow. You have complete control over the number of steps, the layout and the <strong>behavior</strong>. Start with the included <strong><a href="https://octobercms.com/theme/meloncart-fresh">Fresh Theme</a></strong> for a fully working checkout out of the box, or build your own from scratch using theme templates and <strong>components</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="mc-detail-body">

<div class="mc-detail-section">
    <div class="mc-detail-section__text">
        <h2>Single or Multi-Step</h2>
        <p>Choose the checkout structure that works best for your store. Use a single-page <strong>checkout</strong> that keeps everything on one screen, or break it into multiple steps like shipping, payment and review for a more guided experience. Steps can be added, removed or conditionally skipped based on the order context.</p>
        <p>You can also combine related <strong>steps</strong>, like billing and shipping address, into a <strong>single form</strong>. The checkout components are flexible enough to support any layout you design.</p>

        <p>Customers can complete a purchase without creating an account.</p>
        <p>After checkout, they can convert their guest <strong>session</strong> into a full customer account. No friction for first-time buyers, with an easy path to repeat business.</p>

        <p>Coupon codes can be applied during checkout with validation handled automatically. Combined with Meloncart's rule-based discount engine, you can offer percentage discounts, fixed amounts, free shipping or buy-M-get-N <strong>promotions</strong>, all with conditions based on cart contents, customer group or order value.</p>
