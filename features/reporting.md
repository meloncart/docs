# Reporting and Analytics

Meloncart includes built-in reporting tools to help you understand how your store is performing — from high-level sales trends to individual product statistics.

## Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard provides a snapshot of your store's activity: recent orders, revenue trends and key metrics at a glance. Connect an analytics service for additional insights like traffic sources, bounce rates and visitor behavior.

## Sales and Product Reports

Generate visual reports across a range of metrics — revenue, tax collected, shipping charges, top-selling products and most popular categories. Reports can be viewed as line graphs, bar charts or pie charts, and filtered to any date range.

Use filters to drill down further: narrow results by category, manufacturer, shipping country, billing region or specific products to get exactly the data you need.

## Product Statistics

Need a quick performance check on a single product? Each product's detail page includes an overview of total orders and revenue with a visual breakdown over time.

## Data Export

Every report can be exported to CSV for use in spreadsheets, accounting software or business intelligence tools.
