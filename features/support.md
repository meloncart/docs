# Customer Support

Meloncart is backed by responsive, knowledgeable support across multiple channels. Whether you prefer community forums, real-time chat or direct contact, help is available when you need it.

## Support Channels

- **October CMS Support** — access support directly through the October CMS platform
- **Email Helpdesk** — submit a request and get a response from the team
- **GitHub Issues** — report bugs or request features on the project repository
- **Community Forum** — ask questions and share knowledge at talk.octobercms.com
- **Discord** — join the community for real-time discussion and help

## Documentation

This documentation site covers both merchant and developer topics in detail. From store setup and product management through to component APIs and plugin development, the docs are designed to be your first port of call.

## Community

Meloncart benefits from the broader October CMS ecosystem. Tap into an established community of developers, find answers to common questions, and share what you've built.
