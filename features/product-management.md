# Product Management

Meloncart provides a full suite of tools for managing your product catalog — from search and filtering to inventory tracking, bulk operations and image handling. Everything is accessible through the backend admin panel.

## Search and Filter Your Catalog

Quickly find products using the built-in search and filtering tools. Filter by category, manufacturer, product type or custom group to narrow down your catalog, or use the global search to jump straight to what you need.

As your catalog grows, these tools keep product management fast and practical.

## Inventory Control

Track stock levels across your entire catalog and let Meloncart handle the rest. Products can be automatically hidden when they go out of stock, or you can allow back-orders and display expected availability dates to customers.

Stock notifications alert you when inventory runs low, so you can restock before customers notice.

## Import and Export

Migrating from another platform or managing a large catalog in a spreadsheet? Meloncart supports CSV and JSON import for adding products in bulk or updating existing records — pricing, inventory, descriptions and more.

Export your catalog at any time for reporting, backups or integration with external tools.

## Product Images

Each product supports an unlimited number of images. Meloncart's built-in image processing generates thumbnails, handles cropping and provides zoom-ready photos — all rendered however your theme requires.

## Flexible Categories and Groups

Organize products into a hierarchical category tree with unlimited depth. A single product can belong to multiple categories, giving you full control over how your catalog is structured.

Custom groups offer a second layer of organization, independent of categories. Use them to curate collections like "Staff Picks", "New Arrivals" or "Sale Items" — whatever your store needs.

## Variants

Sell products with multiple options — size, color, material — and track each combination independently. Meloncart generates all variant combinations automatically from your option values, and each variant can have its own SKU, price, sale price, weight, barcode and stock level. Variant-specific images update in real time when a customer changes their selection on the storefront.

## Bundles

Create configurable product packages where customers build their own combination. Define slots (e.g. Processor, Memory, Storage), assign products to each slot, and control pricing per choice — default price, fixed override, or percentage discount. Bundles appear as a single item in the cart with a clear price breakdown.

## Options and Extras

Product options define configurable attributes like size or color that customers select before adding to cart. Extras are optional paid add-ons — gift wrapping, extended warranty, engraving — each with their own pricing. Both use reusable sets, so you define them once and apply them across multiple products.

## Tier Pricing

Offer volume-based price breaks that reward larger orders. Set quantity thresholds with discounted prices per tier, and optionally target specific customer groups — so your wholesale accounts see different tiers than retail customers.

## Reviews and Ratings

Customers can leave star ratings and written reviews on products they've purchased. Verified purchase badges are applied automatically, and a helpfulness voting system lets other customers highlight the most useful feedback. You control the moderation workflow — auto-approve or review manually before publishing.

## Sell Anything

Meloncart supports multiple product types out of the box: physical goods, digital downloads, services and subscriptions.

- **Physical products** — full support for weight, dimensions and shipping calculations
- **Digital products** — sell downloadable files with secure download keys, expiration dates and download count tracking
- **Services** — non-shippable items without weight or inventory tracking
- **Subscriptions** — recurring billing on a configurable cycle for any product
