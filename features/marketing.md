# Marketing and Promotion

Meloncart includes built-in tools to help you attract customers, run promotions and maintain a professional brand presence across every customer touchpoint.

## SEO and Custom URLs

Every product, category and page uses clean, customizable URLs. Need a specific landing page for a seasonal promotion or featured category? Assign a dedicated page template to any product or category to create a unique experience without duplicating content.

## Analytics Integration

Connect your analytics platform to track visitor behavior, conversion rates and revenue. Drop your tracking snippet into the theme layout, or use a plugin for deeper integration with services like Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager.

## Email Templates

Customize every transactional email — order confirmations, shipping notifications, password resets — directly from the admin panel. Templates support HTML and CSS layouts with dynamic variables for customer name, order totals, line items and more.

## Discounts and Coupons

Create flexible promotions using Meloncart's rule-based discount engine. Conditions can be combined to target specific scenarios — for example, 10% off orders over $100 shipping to a particular country, or free shipping for VIP customers.

Discounts can apply at the cart level or to individual products, and coupon codes can be generated for use in marketing campaigns.

## Invoices and Packing Slips

Generate branded invoices and packing slips with your company logo, contact details and custom layout. Templates are fully customizable from the admin panel, keeping your brand consistent from the storefront through to the package your customer receives.
