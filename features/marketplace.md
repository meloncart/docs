<div class="mc-features-hero">
    <h1>Marketplace</h1>
    <p>The <strong>October CMS Marketplace</strong> provides a centralized place to find plugins, themes and integrations that extend your Meloncart store. Browse what's available or contribute your own.</p>
</div>

<div class="mc-detail-body">

<div class="mc-detail-section">
    <div class="mc-detail-section__text">
        <h2>Plugins</h2>
        <p>Find additional shipping providers, payment gateways, analytics integrations and other functionality on the Marketplace. Plugins are built by both the core team and <strong>third-party developers</strong>, with new additions published regularly.</p>
        <p>Install plugins directly from the admin panel or via Composer.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="mc-detail-section__visual">
        <img src="/features/feat-marketplace-plugins.png" alt="Plugins" />
    </div>
</div>

<div class="mc-detail-section">
    <div class="mc-detail-section__text">
        <h2>Themes</h2>
        <p>Looking for a starting point rather than building from scratch? Browse themes on the Marketplace to get a ready-made <strong>storefront</strong> that you can customize to fit your needs.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="mc-detail-section__visual">
        <img src="/features/feat-marketplace-themes.png" alt="Themes" />
    </div>
</div>

<div class="mc-detail-section">
    <div class="mc-detail-section__text">
        <h2>Build and Publish</h2>
        <p>The Marketplace is open to developers who want to distribute their own plugins and themes — free or paid. It handles <strong>licensing</strong> and distribution, so you can focus on building.</p>
        <p>If you've built something useful for your own store, consider sharing it with the community.</p>
    </div>
