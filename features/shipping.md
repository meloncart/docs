<div class="mc-detail-section">
    <div class="mc-detail-section__text">
        <h2>Real-Time Carrier Rates</h2>
        <p>Connect to major shipping carriers to provide customers with live, accurate shipping quotes at checkout. Meloncart includes built-in support for:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>UPS</strong></li>
            <li><strong>USPS</strong></li>
            <li><strong>FedEx</strong></li>
            <li><strong>DHL</strong></li>
            <li><strong>Canada Post</strong></li>
            <li><strong>Australia Post</strong></li>
        </ul>
        <p>Each carrier module passes package weight, dimensions and destination to the provider's API and returns real-time rates. Additional carriers can be added through the Marketplace or by building a custom shipping type.</p>
    </div>
